# Hi, I'm Kathan Patel

I'm a Computer Science and Mathematics student at the University of Wisconsin–Madison interested in quantitative research, machine learning, and software engineering.

I build systems that connect mathematical ideas with practical applications, from financial research and risk management to sports analytics. I care about reproducibility, rigorous evaluation, and clearly documented limitations.

## Featured projects

### [SentientAlpha](https://github.com/kathan17-source/SentientAlpha)

Auditable multi-agent quantitative research and simulation platform (v0.12.0).

- Point-in-time data, machine learning and sentiment research
- Portfolio optimization, independent risk controls and backtesting
- Persistent simulated trading with inspectable decisions and accounting
- **Python · FastAPI · Next.js · TypeScript**

Research and simulation only; robust financial alpha has not been demonstrated.

### [CourtIQ](https://github.com/kathan17-source/courtiq)

Full-stack tennis analytics with separate ATP/WTA models, surface-aware Elo, chronological research, match probabilities, Monte Carlo simulation and 2D movement analysis.

**Python · FastAPI · JavaScript** · [Live demo](https://courtiq-77cz.onrender.com/)

Current ATP evaluation provenance is under review; working inference does not establish validated accuracy. Single-camera 2D movement estimates are not clinical biomechanics.

### [PathFinder](https://github.com/kathan17-source/PathFinder)

Physics Olympiad problem-navigation website built with **C# · ASP.NET Web Forms · .NET Framework**. Topic pages organize problem references and externally hosted solution links; solution coverage is incomplete and external availability varies.
